# Canvas Design

Create one polished visual artifact with a clear compositional idea.

## Approach

Form a compact internal direction before producing the artifact:

- spatial attitude and hierarchy
- limited color logic
- material or texture cues
- typography role
- text budget
- output size and medium

Do not turn that direction into a separate deliverable unless the user asks for concepts or rationale.

Choose the simplest medium that gives the required control:

- image generation for bitmap-first artwork
- SVG for crisp vector compositions
- HTML/CSS or canvas for layout-driven/editorial work
- PDF when print output is required

Use bundled fonts from `assets/fonts/` when they fit; do not fetch assets merely for novelty.

## Quality

- Communicate through space, rhythm, scale, contrast and material before explanatory text.
- Keep text sparse unless the brief requires information density.
- Borrow principles, never a living artist's recognizable signature.
- Prefer one dominant idea over decorative accumulation.
- Verify canvas bounds, margins, alignment, legibility, contrast and typography.
- Refine once after inspection, changing only what materially improves coherence.

If the artifact becomes part of a product interface, use it only as source artwork or visual direction and implement the UI with `frontend-developer`. Do not force a poster workflow onto product UI.
